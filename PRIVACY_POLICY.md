# Privacy Policy for Qurbani App

**Last updated:** November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Qurbani App** ("we", "us", or "our", package: `com.aivion.qurbani`) collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application and related online services.

## 1. Information We Collect
When you create an account, browse livestock, book sacrificial shares, or manage orders, we may collect:
- **Account Details:** Full name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
- **Order & Transaction Data:** Details of animals selected, share quantities, order timestamps, and payment reference identifiers.
- **Device & Usage Data:** Basic device telemetry, operating system version, and anonymous crash analytics to keep the app secure and stable.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To process, fulfill, and track your livestock and Qurbani share bookings.
- To coordinate timely slaughter, butchering, and meat delivery with verified personnel.
- To communicate order confirmation, status updates, and essential service alerts.
- To prevent fraudulent bookings and ensure platform security.

## 3. Data Protection and Security
We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All data transmissions between the application and our servers are encrypted via HTTPS / TLS.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared strictly with necessary operational providers (such as cloud hosting and database infrastructure) to deliver our core services.

## 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion
We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as required by law. Users may request account deletion or data removal at any time by contacting us.
